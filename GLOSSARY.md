# Glossary

**Member**: One of the two people using the app (Sara, Vinicius), identified by their Letterboxd username.

**Watchlist**: The public Letterboxd watchlist of one Member. Source of truth; the app never edits it.

**Combined Watchlist**: The union of both Members' Watchlists. Each Film in it carries its Owners. Films already watched by one Member stay in it.

**Owners**: Which Members have a Film in their Watchlist: Sara, Vinicius, or both.

**Film**: A movie as identified by its TMDB id; title, genres, year, runtime, country, language and rating come from TMDB.

**Provider**: A service where a Film can be watched in Brazil (e.g. Netflix, Apple TV, Google Play). A service resold through another one (e.g. "HBO Max Amazon Channel") counts as its base Provider (HBO Max).

**Offer**: A Film being available on a Provider in Brazil, with one Offer Type.

**Offer Type**: How an Offer is accessed: subscription, free, ads, rent or buy. All types count as "available".

**Sync**: Refreshing the Watchlists and Film/Offer data from Letterboxd and TMDB.
